# FO76 vanilla UI — quick rules

> Load full `SKILL.md` when executing this workflow.

- Pin the exact game, archive, SWF, language, and platform build.
- Cite evidence for every display-list and provider claim.
- Treat public names as search keys, not vanilla schemas.
- Prefer a HUD Mod Loader child movie for new UI.
- Keep a clean original when changing a vanilla movie.
- Do not treat archive readability or an `End` tag as compatibility.
- Prove coordinate basis, safe area, scale, and aspect behavior.
- Measure layout under wrapping, resize, and localization.
- Consume input only while the widget owns focus.
- Preserve unhandled gameplay and text-entry events.
- Pair text-edit start/end across cancel, reload, and focus loss.
- Bound retained rows and avoid unproven per-frame work.
- Report `compatible`, `needs-review`, `incompatible`, or `unknown` with evidence.
- Keep ESM and game files out of tracked fixtures.
